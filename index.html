Private Offline Vault

Drop .mp3, .mp4, .wav, .m4a, .ogg, or .flac files anywhere onto the window. MP3 Wanda automatically reads embedded ID3 title, artist, lyrics, and cover art, and stores audio locally in IndexedDB for zero-internet playback.

Synced Lyrics & Live Stamper

Open Studio → Lyrics to import .vtt or .lrc files, or type lyrics manually. While editing, tap + Stamp during playback to insert exact timestamps so lyrics highlight and auto-scroll cleanly in time with the track.

Visual 5-Band EQ & Atmosphere

Shape your sound in Studio → EQ with a live frequency curve and 6 tuned presets. Switch to Atmosphere to gently blend procedural Soft Rain, Vinyl Crackle, Brown Noise, or Warm Hearth beneath any song.

Focus View & JSON Backups

Click the album artwork in the bottom player (or press F) for a calm full-screen Focus View with live waveform and lyrics. Open Vault Ready anytime to export or restore your playlists, lyrics, and listening stats as a private JSON file.
